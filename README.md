# AXIGNAL

**Global Opportunity Intelligence & Operations**

AXIGNAL is a global platform for discovering, investigating and operating professional opportunities. It connects governed evidence across procurement, grants, regulation, infrastructure, corporate activity, macroeconomics, trade, energy and innovation; then preserves the work required to pursue each opportunity through its outcome and learning.

## Product chain

```text
signals
→ admitted sources
→ Evidence Objects
→ Candidate Claims
→ deterministic admission
→ InvestigationContext
→ Opportunity
→ Pursuit
→ Operational Workspace
→ Outcome
→ Learning
```

AI may discover, extract, classify and propose. It cannot admit canonical truth, grant product authority, approve a customer's operational decision or authorise launch.

## Required libraries

Seven foundational libraries support nine opportunity libraries:

- Global Public Procurement;
- Grants and Non-Dilutive Funding;
- Regulatory and Policy Demand;
- Infrastructure and Capital Projects;
- Corporate, Filings and Ownership Signals;
- Sovereign, Macro and Public Investment;
- Trade, Supply Chain and Market Flows;
- Energy and Climate Transition;
- Innovation, Research and Intellectual Property.

Each library has independent source, rights, quality, lifecycle, privacy, entitlement, workspace, observability and rollback gates.

## Identity, trial and seats

The candidate stack contains:

- WebAuthn passkeys;
- opaque revocable sessions;
- secure recovery;
- tenant-level trial-abuse governance;
- seven days beginning at first admitted AI use;
- 1,000,000 trial tokens;
- two trial seats;
- three Professional seats;
- fifteen Team seats;
- transactional seat reservations and roles.

Public signup, production SMTP, production bot verification, live Stripe and commercial activation remain blocked.
