## Scope

A decision may authorise only the private, bounded O01 evidence campaign while TED remains `CANDIDATE`.

It does not authorise:

- TED product admission;
- public claims or launch;
- public redistribution;
- contact marketing;
- model training or fine-tuning;
- bid submission or offer presentation;
- external notification delivery;
- any other field, query, sample, threshold or retention change.

The v0.2 decisions cannot be reused because they explicitly do not survive a field-set change.
