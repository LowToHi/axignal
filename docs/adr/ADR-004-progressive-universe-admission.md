# ADR-004 — Progressive Opportunity-Universe Admission

- Status: `ACCEPTED`
- Date: `2026-07-26`

## Context

The long-term product vision spans public contracts, grants, business opportunities, real assets, listed instruments, private companies, trade, macroeconomics, crypto, regulation and emerging technology.

Launching all universes simultaneously would create shallow coverage, inconsistent semantics, uncontrolled data rights and misleading comparability.

## Decision

AXIGNAL will use one shared epistemic infrastructure while admitting each opportunity universe independently.

A universe is public and commercially supported only after passing gates for:

- data rights;
- coverage;
- ontology;
- freshness;
- claim admission;
- regulatory boundary;
- product workflow;
- buyer value;
- cost and gross margin;
- outage and revocation behaviour.

The initial commercial wedge should use a small combination of official macroeconomic data, European procurement or non-dilutive capital, public-company disclosures and regulation-created business signals, subject to validation.

## Consequences

- The product may display experimental universes with explicit coverage labels.
- No implementation agent may equate architectural support with commercial coverage.
- Pricing entitlements are universe-aware.
- Every additional universe repeats the admission process.
- Common scoring dimensions may be reused, but universe-specific methodologies remain separate.

## Alternatives considered

- Launch a broad aggregator immediately.
- Restrict AXIGNAL permanently to one vertical.
- Use a single universal opportunity score and ontology.
