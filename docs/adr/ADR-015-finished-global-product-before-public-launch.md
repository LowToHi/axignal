# ADR-015 — Finished Global Product Before Public Launch

Status: `PROPOSED / IMPLEMENTATION REQUIRED`
Date: `2026-07-30`
Goal ID: `AXIGNAL-GOAL-001`
Governing contract: `docs/contracts/30-global-e2e-development-contract-v1.4.md`
Supersedes: the active sequencing parts of ADR-013 and any partial-launch interpretation of F12

## Context

AXIGNAL's current baseline proves a governed vertical slice and a bounded procurement runtime. It also proves trial, entitlement, retention and deterministic internal billing paths. The existing roadmap, however, still treats worldwide source expansion and several major product capabilities as post-launch work.

The authority human has decided that AXIGNAL will not launch as a consultative information layer, a TED-only product or an incomplete global promise.

A finished product requires:

- global foundational libraries;
- multiple opportunity libraries;
- Opportunity Operations;
- specialised workspaces;
- cross-library intelligence;
- multilingual operation;
- enterprise and commercial runtimes;
- production acceptance;
- evidence of payment, retention and margin.

## Decision

AXIGNAL adopts Contract 30 and the P00–P24 programme as the active development authority.

The public-launch gate is fail-closed. There is no partial launch.

Procurement remains the first implementation library, but no longer defines AXIGNAL's identity or authorises an earlier narrow public launch.

The worldwide procurement catalogue and the new O02–O09 catalogues are mandatory development programmes. Catalogue inclusion does not admit a source.

Opportunity Operations is required. AXIGNAL must support the path from evidence-governed opportunity discovery to pursuit, work, outcome and learning inside the platform.

## Consequences

### Positive

- brand identity and product scope align;
- global ambition becomes executable rather than rhetorical;
- the product retains users through operational value;
- library and source work becomes independently governed;
- public copy will describe a finished product;
- cross-library value and expansion revenue become possible.

### Negative

- launch is substantially later;
- source-rights, adapter and maintenance cost increases;
- nine operational verticals require disciplined shared architecture;
- user research, multilingual QA and enterprise work become mandatory before launch;
- the programme can fail closed if global coverage or economics are not supportable.

## Alternatives rejected

### Launch Procurement first and expand later

Rejected by the authority human. It would launch an incomplete product and risk fixing AXIGNAL's identity as a tender platform.

### Launch a consultative intelligence layer without operations

Rejected. Users would move claims and dossiers into other tools, reducing recurrence and leaving a major workflow gap.

### Claim global coverage from catalogue breadth

Rejected. Discovery catalogues are not source admission or product coverage.

### Build one universal scraper or universal normaliser

Rejected. Rights, taxonomies, identifiers and lifecycle semantics are source-specific.

## Authority boundary

This ADR does not:

- admit any source;
- enable public billing;
- set any candidate price as validated;
- authorise a global-coverage claim;
- grant AI canonical authority;
- enable private customer documents;
- accept P00 or any later phase.

## Validation obligations

ADR-015 advances only through:

- Contract Validation;
- typed P00–P24 tasks;
- Library Registry;
- source-catalogue index;
- dependency graph;
- evidence-backed phase gates;
- final P24 disposition.

## Rollback

If superseded, the repository must preserve this ADR, Contract 30, tasks and evidence as history. A new ADR must explicitly select the replacement scope and launch rule.
