# 00 — Product Constitution

Version: `0.1.0`
Status: `NORMATIVE / FOUNDATION`

## 1. Product thesis

AXIGNAL MUST be built as a premium global observation and exploration system for economic opportunity.

It MUST continuously transform heterogeneous external signals into:

1. structured observations;
2. atomic claims;
3. explicit evidence and provenance;
4. contradictions and unknowns;
5. temporal market states;
6. scenario distributions;
7. explorable opportunity structures.

AXIGNAL MUST NOT be reduced to a chatbot, newsletter, generic dashboard, idea generator or trading terminal.

## 2. Category

Canonical category:

> **Global Opportunity Intelligence**

Canonical descriptor:

> **The global climate system for economic opportunity.**

Canonical principle:

> **Signals reveal change. Claims establish evidence. AXIGNAL maps opportunity.**

## 3. Buyer

The primary buyer is an **active multi-asset wealth operator** who has meaningful capital, consumes multiple specialist platforms and lacks a unified institutional research infrastructure.

Priority segments:

1. entrepreneurs and executives with investible liquidity;
2. sophisticated independent investors;
3. small family offices;
4. independent advisers and research boutiques;
5. venture studios, holdings and corporate-development teams.

The initial product MUST be B2B or professional-user oriented. Consumer mass-market positioning is out of scope for the foundation release.

## 4. User problem

The user’s primary scarcity is not raw information. It is:

- attention;
- cross-market comparability;
- source traceability;
- understanding of temporal change;
- visibility into contradictions;
- confidence that a finding was not improvised by a generative model;
- access to adjacent opportunity classes outside the user’s habitual platforms.

AXIGNAL MUST optimise for **decision preparation**, not decision substitution.

## 5. Product promise

AXIGNAL MUST help the user answer:

- What is changing?
- Where is the evidence strengthening or weakening?
- Which markets, sectors, geographies or assets are connected?
- What does the historical record show?
- Which scenarios are plausible under explicit assumptions?
- What contradicts the opportunity thesis?
- What would invalidate it?
- How fresh and legally usable is the underlying information?

AXIGNAL MUST NOT answer “what should I personally buy?” in the foundation scope.

## 6. Product invariants

### 6.1 Structured before conversational

The structured graph, ledger and scenario system are authoritative. Conversational interfaces MAY query and explain them but MUST NOT invent canonical product state.

### 6.2 Epistemic transparency

Every material statement MUST be classified as one of:

- `OBSERVED`
- `CALCULATED`
- `INFERRED`
- `PREDICTED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `CONTESTED`
- `FALSIFIED`
- `EXPIRED`

### 6.3 Contradictions are first-class

Every opportunity MUST expose supporting, contradicting and unresolved claims. The system MUST NOT optimise only for positive evidence.

### 6.4 Temporal validity

Every claim MUST have observation time, relevant event time and validity rules. “Latest” MUST always be source-relative.

### 6.5 Deterministic governance

The platform MAY use probabilistic extraction and modelling, but canonical admission MUST be governed by explicit, versioned rules.

### 6.6 No hidden personal recommendation

The platform MAY allow objective filters such as geography, sector, ticket band, liquidity, horizon and evidence strength. It MUST NOT silently transform those filters into a representation that an instrument is personally suitable for a specific user.

### 6.7 Progressive coverage

No market universe is considered supported until it passes its admission gate. Breadth MUST NOT be purchased with superficiality.

## 7. Product surfaces

### AXIGNAL Globe

World-scale geographic exploration using heatmaps, layers, clusters and temporal playback.

### AXIGNAL Explorer

Cross-market discovery and filtering by geography, sector, asset class, event, evidence, maturity and scenario.

### AXIGNAL Atlas

Graph-based navigation of entities, markets, claims, regulations, technologies, assets and transmission paths.

### AXIGNAL Climate

Market-state and opportunity-state views showing momentum, evidence density, contradiction pressure, persistence and scenario drift.

### AXIGNAL Claims

Auditable claim detail including evidence, provenance, method, versions, dependencies, conflicts and expiry.

### AXIGNAL Watchlists

User-selected observation scopes. Watchlists represent interests, not personalised recommendations.

### AXIGNAL API

Versioned access to authorised claims, opportunities, aggregates, scenarios and metadata according to plan entitlements and source rights.

## 8. Opportunity universes

The architecture MUST support multiple isolated universes with shared epistemic infrastructure:

1. business opportunities;
2. public contracts and non-dilutive capital;
3. real assets and property;
4. private companies and digital assets;
5. trade and supply chains;
6. macroeconomic and sovereign context;
7. listed instruments and market data;
8. crypto and on-chain data;
9. regulation-created demand;
10. emerging technology and crowdfunding.

Each universe MUST define its own ontology extensions, source rights, freshness model, risk classification and acceptance gates.

## 9. Initial wedge

The foundation architecture is global and multiverse. The first commercial release SHOULD use only a small set of high-quality universes.

Recommended initial combination:

- global macro and sovereign context;
- public procurement and non-dilutive capital in Europe;
- business and technology opportunity signals;
- selected public-company disclosures;
- a limited real-asset or property dataset where licensing permits.

Listed-instrument price recommendations, crypto execution and personalised portfolio tooling MUST remain disabled.

## 10. Success definition

The leading product metric is:

> **Meaningful decisions whose research process starts or materially advances inside AXIGNAL.**

Supporting metrics:

- weekly qualified active users;
- claims inspected per qualified session;
- opportunity-to-source drill-down rate;
- watchlist retention;
- scenario revisit rate;
- paid conversion;
- annual-plan conversion;
- source coverage quality;
- claim correction rate;
- trust and methodology comprehension;
- renewal and expansion revenue.

Raw page views, chat messages and generated text volume MUST NOT be treated as primary success metrics.

## 11. Non-goals

Foundation AXIGNAL MUST NOT:

- act as a broker, exchange or custodian;
- execute or route transactions;
- manage or rebalance portfolios;
- promise returns;
- claim deterministic economic outcomes;
- offer social trading or copy trading;
- become a content farm;
- publish untraceable AI-generated opportunities;
- ingest all publicly visible platforms without rights review;
- launch all universes simultaneously.

## 12. Validation posture

This constitution represents the strongest current product hypothesis. It MUST be iterated through evidence.

No part of this document authorises false claims of product-market fit, predictive accuracy or regulatory immunity.
