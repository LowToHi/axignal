# 01 — Business Model and Pricing Contract

Version: `0.1.0`
Status: `NORMATIVE / HYPOTHESIS-BOUND`

## 1. Commercial model

AXIGNAL MUST be sold as a premium recurring intelligence product, not as a low-cost newsletter, generic AI assistant or transaction commission business.

The commercial stack MAY include:

1. public research and selected opportunity climate pages;
2. paid SaaS subscriptions;
3. annual professional plans;
4. team and family-office plans;
5. API and data entitlements;
6. one-off research dossiers or opportunity drops;
7. private-data integration for enterprise customers.

Transaction execution, custody and performance fees are outside the foundation scope.

## 2. Value unit

The paid value is not “number of ideas”. The paid value is:

- time saved across fragmented sources;
- cross-market visibility;
- traceability from opportunity to evidence;
- explicit contradiction and uncertainty;
- historical replay;
- structured scenario monitoring;
- discovery of adjacent assets and markets;
- institutional-quality research workflow without an institutional research team.

## 3. Buyer and willingness to pay

The priority user is not defined only by net worth. The buyer MUST satisfy at least two of these conditions:

- makes recurring decisions across more than one asset or opportunity class;
- actively uses several specialist data or research platforms;
- controls or advises on material capital;
- has high opportunity cost for research time;
- values source auditability and historical evidence;
- can convert one relevant insight into value exceeding the annual subscription.

## 4. Packaging hypothesis

Pricing below is a testable hypothesis, not validated fact.

### Explorer — €99/month

Target: sophisticated individual operators.

Entitlements:

- selected universes;
- Globe and Explorer;
- basic watchlists;
- principal claims and source references;
- delayed or standard-frequency updates;
- limited history;
- no API;
- no custom data integration.

### Professional — €299/month

Target: active investors, entrepreneurs, advisers and research professionals.

Entitlements:

- all generally released universes;
- supporting and contradicting claims;
- scenario views;
- full available history;
- advanced filters;
- exports within source rights;
- higher watchlist and alert limits;
- professional reporting.

### Wealth Intelligence — €799/month

Target: small family offices, holdings and small teams.

Entitlements:

- multi-user workspace;
- shared watchlists and annotations;
- expanded history and comparisons;
- limited API quota;
- higher-frequency alerts where source latency permits;
- organisation-level research collections;
- premium audit and export controls.

### Enterprise — from €1,500/month

Target: family offices, consultancies, venture studios, corporate development and research teams.

Entitlements MAY include:

- negotiated seats;
- API and data feed;
- SSO and SCIM;
- private workspaces;
- customer-owned data connectors;
- contractual security and support terms;
- custom universes;
- data residency options;
- enterprise audit logs.

## 5. Annual billing

The system SHOULD privilege annual commitments after initial validation.

Recommended annual discount hypothesis:

- Explorer: 2 months equivalent discount;
- Professional: 2 months equivalent discount;
- Wealth Intelligence: negotiated annual commitment;
- Enterprise: annual contract by default.

Discounting MUST NOT become the primary conversion mechanism. The core conversion mechanism is demonstrated value and trusted methodology.

## 6. Revenue architecture example

A diversified path to approximately €100,000 monthly recurring revenue is preferable to dependence on exactly 1,000 identical subscribers.

Illustrative composition:

| Segment | Accounts | Monthly price | Monthly revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Explorer | 400 | €99 | €39,600 |
| Professional | 100 | €299 | €29,900 |
| Wealth Intelligence | 25 | €799 | €19,975 |
| Enterprise | 5 | €2,500 average | €12,500 |
| **Total** | **530** | — | **€101,975** |

This table is a scenario, not a forecast.

## 7. Acquisition model

AXIGNAL SHOULD avoid broad consumer-performance advertising as the initial channel.

Priority acquisition channels:

- flagship research reports;
- invite-only demonstrations;
- referrals;
- independent wealth advisers;
- tax and legal firms serving entrepreneurs;
- family-office and investment communities;
- enterprise research partnerships;
- cited public data pages;
- founder-led authority and methodology publication.

The free surface MUST demonstrate the method without reproducing the entire paid product.

## 8. Onboarding contract

The onboarding MUST configure observation interests rather than personal suitability.

Allowed initial preferences:

- geographies;
- sectors;
- opportunity universes;
- asset classes;
- ticket bands;
- liquidity bands;
- time horizons;
- update frequency;
- evidence thresholds;
- currencies and languages.

The foundation onboarding MUST NOT collect risk tolerance, required return, complete portfolio composition or other data for the purpose of generating personalised investment recommendations.

## 9. Pricing gates

A plan or price change MUST be supported by evidence from at least one of:

- paid conversion test;
- customer interview with explicit budget responsibility;
- renewal data;
- feature-level usage and retention;
- willingness-to-pay experiment;
- enterprise proposal outcome.

Pricing MUST remain configurable and versioned. Public price changes MUST preserve existing customer entitlements according to explicit migration rules.

## 10. Commercial gates

### Gate B0 — Problem evidence

Pass when at least 20 qualified target users confirm recurring cross-platform research fragmentation and at least 5 describe a recent decision where AXIGNAL-like evidence would have had material value.

### Gate B1 — Paid prototype

Pass when at least 10 non-affiliated qualified users pay for access to a narrow, manually supported prototype.

### Gate B2 — Retention

Pass when at least 60% of the first qualified paid cohort remains active and paid after 90 days, or an equivalent annual-commitment signal is observed.

### Gate B3 — Expansion

Pass when at least 15% of retained accounts upgrade, add seats or purchase an additional data entitlement.

### Gate B4 — Repeatable acquisition

Pass when a defined channel produces paying customers with contribution margin and acceptable payback under the approved financial model.

## 11. Cost discipline

Before meaningful revenue, AXIGNAL MUST prioritise:

- public and contractually reusable data;
- deterministic transforms;
- caching;
- incremental processing;
- local or low-cost models;
- sampled rather than universal AI enrichment.

Paid model usage MUST be metered by workflow, customer plan and expected product value.

## 12. Revenue-gated AI

The platform SHOULD maintain an AI budget ledger:

```text
confirmed revenue
→ reserves and taxes
→ infrastructure budget
→ model-usage budget
→ workflow-level ROI review
```

High-cost enrichment MUST NOT run universally when deterministic or cached methods satisfy the contract.

## 13. Refund and expectation control

Product marketing MUST NOT promise profits or suitable investment outcomes.

Customer-facing terms SHOULD state that the subscription grants access to information, methodology and software functionality. Service availability, data latency and source-dependent limitations MUST be described precisely.

## 14. Primary commercial metrics

- qualified trial-to-paid conversion;
- monthly and annual recurring revenue;
- gross margin by plan;
- net revenue retention;
- logo retention;
- paid-account weekly activity;
- watchlist revisit rate;
- opportunity-to-evidence drill-down;
- report export or share rate;
- enterprise sales-cycle duration;
- data and AI cost per active paid account.

## 15. Kill conditions

The model MUST be reconsidered if, after a properly executed narrow validation:

- qualified users consume content but refuse to pay;
- retention depends primarily on constant manual consulting;
- data licensing makes gross margin structurally unattractive;
- users treat the platform as entertainment rather than a decision workflow;
- the product cannot establish trust without regulated personalised advice;
- a general AI provider can reproduce the paid value without AXIGNAL’s proprietary data history and workflow.
