# 11 — Product Hypothesis Register

Version: `0.1.0`
Status: `NORMATIVE / EXPERIMENTAL CONTROL`

## 1. Purpose

AXIGNAL’s architecture can be specified before the market is proven. Its buyer, pricing, first universe, acquisition model and predictive value cannot.

This register prevents hypotheses from becoming undocumented assumptions.

## 2. Hypothesis states

- `PROPOSED`
- `TEST_DESIGNED`
- `TESTING`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `CONTESTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `SUPERSEDED`

A hypothesis may be `SUPPORTED` without being universally true. Every decision MUST state its evidence and scope.

## 3. H-001 — Buyer

**Claim:** The best initial buyer is an active multi-asset operator, entrepreneur, adviser or small family office that lacks institutional research infrastructure.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Evidence required: at least 20 qualified interviews and 10 independent paid design partners.
- Falsification: strong interest without payment; need limited to bespoke consulting; buyer already satisfied by existing institutional platforms.

## 4. H-002 — Core value

**Claim:** Cross-market synthesis, traceability and historical claim evolution create more paid value than raw data breadth or conversational answers.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: compare product variants emphasising evidence drill-down, broad data coverage and AI explanation.
- Primary measures: paid conversion, retention, source drill-down, watchlist revisit and qualitative decision impact.

## 5. H-003 — Exploration habit

**Claim:** A spatial and temporal exploration interface can become a recurring workflow comparable to checking a weather system before making plans.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: qualified-user prototype sessions and longitudinal paid usage.
- Falsification: users consistently bypass maps and graphs for static reports or exports.

## 6. H-004 — Entry pricing

**Claim:** €99/month is a viable entry price for a sophisticated individual tier and does not materially damage premium perception.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Competing hypotheses: €149 or €299 minimum; invite-only annual access; no low tier.
- Test: willingness-to-pay and paid conversion with identical core value framing.

## 7. H-005 — Tier architecture

**Claim:** A four-tier model of €99, €299, €799 and enterprise from €1,500/month can achieve approximately €100,000 MRR with fewer than 1,000 accounts.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Note: the revenue table is arithmetic, not a demand forecast.
- Evidence required: conversion, retention, expansion and gross-margin data by tier.

## 8. H-006 — Initial universe

**Claim:** The strongest initial combination is official macro/sovereign context plus European procurement, non-dilutive capital, public-company disclosures and regulation-created business demand.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test dimensions: rights, coverage, buyer value, frequency, differentiation, acquisition relevance, operating cost and regulatory complexity.
- Falsification: no coherent recurring user workflow or insufficient willingness to pay.

## 9. H-007 — Claims graph defence

**Claim:** A versioned graph connecting claims, evidence, contradictions, forecasts and outcomes will become a meaningful defence against general AI providers.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Evidence required: improving retrieval and calibration from proprietary history; user preference for AXIGNAL state over fresh general-AI research; switching cost from watchlists and historical context.
- Falsification: users value only fresh summarisation and do not inspect or revisit structured state.

## 10. H-008 — Deterministic admission

**Claim:** Deterministic structural, rights and epistemic gates materially improve trust and data quality compared with direct model publication.

- State: `SUPPORTED BY PRIOR TECHNICAL THESIS / NOT YET VALIDATED IN AXIGNAL`
- Test: controlled candidate-claim evaluation measuring false admission, traceability, correction cost and user trust.

## 11. H-009 — Regulatory boundary

**Claim:** Material user value can be delivered as observation and general research without personalised recommendations, execution or custody.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: user research, legal review and paid usage.
- Falsification: qualified users will only pay when AXIGNAL tells them exactly what to buy or allocates their portfolio.

## 12. H-010 — Source economics

**Claim:** Initial public and selectively licensed sources can support an attractive gross margin before expensive institutional data is required.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: source-by-source cost and value ledger.
- Falsification: essential user value requires licences that cannot be supported by validated pricing.

## 13. H-011 — Revenue-gated AI

**Claim:** Deterministic processing, caching and local models can support foundation quality, while paid frontier-model usage can be limited to revenue-backed high-value workflows.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: quality/cost comparison by workflow.
- Falsification: acceptable claim extraction or explanation requires universal high-cost inference.

## 14. H-012 — Global architecture, selective commercialisation

**Claim:** AXIGNAL can maintain global data architecture while launching commercially by admitted jurisdiction and universe.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: source and entitlement isolation; jurisdiction-specific legal review; customer demand by geography.

## 15. H-013 — Brand

**Claim:** AXIGNAL and `axignal.com` can communicate an authoritative, international and multiverse intelligence product.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Required evidence: domain control, trademark screening, pronunciation tests, buyer perception and visual-identity testing.
- Repository slug `axignal` is not evidence of public-brand validity.

## 16. Experiment record requirements

Every hypothesis experiment MUST record:

- hypothesis ID;
- population and recruitment;
- date;
- method;
- predeclared success threshold;
- results;
- limitations;
- cost;
- decision activated;
- new state;
- follow-up hypothesis.

## 17. Governance

- A product feature MUST NOT cite a `PROPOSED` hypothesis as proven.
- Public marketing MUST not overstate supported scope.
- Rejected hypotheses remain in history.
- Pricing and universe decisions MUST link to this register.
- The register MUST be reviewed at every major commercial gate.
