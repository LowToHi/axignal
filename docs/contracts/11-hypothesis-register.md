# 11 — Product Hypothesis Register

Version: `0.3.0`
Status: `NORMATIVE / EXPERIMENTAL CONTROL`

## 1. Purpose

AXIGNAL’s architecture can be specified before the market is proven. Its buyer, pricing, first universe, acquisition model, trial design, global-source value and predictive value cannot.

This register prevents hypotheses from becoming undocumented assumptions.

## 2. Hypothesis states

- `PROPOSED`
- `TEST_DESIGNED`
- `TESTING`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `CONTESTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `SUPERSEDED`

A hypothesis may be `SUPPORTED` without being universally true. Every decision MUST state its evidence and scope.

## 3. H-001 — Initial B2G buyer

**Claim:** The strongest initial buyer is an organisation or professional that sells to government and has recurring cost in finding, qualifying, investigating or monitoring public-procurement opportunities.

Priority candidates:

- European SMEs and growth suppliers;
- bid and tender teams;
- public-sector sales teams;
- procurement and market-entry consultancies;
- contractors, technology suppliers, engineering and specialised-service firms;
- corporate market-intelligence teams.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Evidence required: at least 20 qualified interviews, five recent costly or missed-opportunity cases and 10 independent paid Design Partners or an approved equivalent annual-contract signal.
- Falsification: strong interest without payment; demand limited to commodity alerts; need depends on bespoke consulting; users cannot complete the evidence workflow; or the buyer only values automated bid execution outside AXIGNAL’s scope.
- Supersession: the previous multi-asset operator and family-office-first hypothesis remains historical but is superseded for the first commercial procurement wedge by ADR-012, ADR-013 and Contract 28.

## 4. H-002 — Core value

**Claim:** Source-linked synthesis, lifecycle reconstruction, contradiction visibility and persistent dossiers create more paid value than raw notice breadth or conversational answers.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Test: compare product variants emphasising evidence drill-down, broad alert coverage and AI explanation.
- Primary measures: paid conversion, retention, source drill-down, dossier completion and revisit, monitored-opportunity use and qualitative decision impact.
- Falsification: users consistently pay only for commodity alerts or exports and do not inspect, share or revisit structured evidence.

## 5. H-003 — Exploration habit

**Claim:** A spatial, relational and temporal exploration interface can become a recurring B2G workflow for scanning procurement demand, buyer behaviour and opportunity change.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: qualified-user prototype sessions and longitudinal paid usage.
- Falsification: users consistently bypass Globe, Graph and Timeline for static lists and dossiers, with no decision or retention benefit from the shared InvestigationContext.

## 6. H-004 — Professional entry-price sweet spot

**Claim:** A Professional price between **€349 and €499 per month** can remain reachable for a qualified SME, bid professional or consultancy while avoiding the low-value signal of a commodity tender-alert SaaS.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Competing hypotheses: lower constrained discovery tier; €599–€799 minimum; annual-only access; sales-led Team plan as the true entry product.
- Test: paid Design Partner offers and willingness-to-pay experiments using a working evidence-linked dossier, with identical product-authority framing.
- Required measures: paid acceptance, activation, repeated use, gross margin, support burden, renewal, annual commitment and premium-perception comprehension.
- Falsification: no qualified paid commitment; margin structurally fails; buyers interpret the band as too expensive for the value; or lowering price materially improves retained paid use without damaging positioning.

## 7. H-005 — B2G tier architecture

**Claim:** A commercial ladder of paid Design Partner, Professional, Team / Growth and Enterprise can capture different procurement workflows without reducing value to tokens or feature-count inflation.

Candidate bands:

- Design Partner: `€300–€600/month` per organisation for a fixed validation term;
- Professional: `€349–€499/month`;
- Team / Growth: `€899–€1,499/month`;
- Enterprise: initial range `€18,000–€45,000/year`, adjusted for seats, API, private connectors, security, support, data and services.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Note: these bands are hypotheses, not established market prices or a revenue forecast.
- Evidence required: conversion, activation, retention, expansion, support and gross-margin data by plan; every plan row maps to a real server-side entitlement.
- Falsification: buyers cannot distinguish the tiers; Team value is explained only by artificial limits; Enterprise delivery obligations overwhelm price; or one simpler package outperforms the ladder.

## 8. H-006 — Initial universe

**Claim:** The strongest first commercial wedge is **European Public Procurement Intelligence**, beginning with the official TED source family; macroeconomic and trade data may later act as context layers, while grants and regulation-created demand remain adjacent universes subject to independent admission.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Technical evidence state: `ADVANCED / NOT PRODUCT ADMISSION`.
- Selection evidence: versioned comparative scorecard `AX-F8-UNIVERSE-001`; European procurement ranked first at `96/100` and passed all declared selection knockouts.
- Technical evidence: a bounded TED Search API probe, eForms SDK 1.14.2 parser, deterministic sandbox admission, correction/cancellation/result lineage, 50 provisional Evidence Objects and a traceable dossier exist on the canonical baseline.
- Decision: ADR-012 selects the implementation wedge; ADR-013 selects the candidate B2G commercial expression. Neither admits TED as a product source nor establishes user demand.
- Bounded workflow: discover public demand by geography, CPV, buyer, value and deadline; inspect notice and award evidence, changes, cancellations, contradictions, missing fields and historical relationships; save and monitor the investigation.
- Predeclared next evidence: exact TED source admission; authenticated tenant-scoped persistent ResearchRun; one governed end-to-end dossier in InvestigationContext; qualified workflow evidence; one paid event or signed Design Partner commitment; acceptable source and compute cost; zero critical rights, privacy, security or regulatory defects.
- Falsification: source-specific rights fail; the workflow requires unauthorised portal scraping; completeness cannot support the use case; privacy minimisation destroys essential value; qualified users want only commodity alerts; operating cost makes target margin structurally unattractive; or value requires bid execution or misleading certainty.
- Current boundary: `WEDGE_SELECTED / TECHNICAL_EVIDENCE_READY / SOURCE_NOT_PRODUCT_ADMITTED / UNIVERSE_NOT_SUPPORTED / PRODUCTION_RUNTIME_DISABLED / PUBLIC_MARKETING_RESTRICTED`.

## 9. H-007 — Claims graph defence

**Claim:** A versioned graph connecting procurement notices, buyers, suppliers, lots, awards, claims, evidence, contradictions, forecasts and outcomes will become a meaningful defence against general AI providers.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Evidence required: improving retrieval and lifecycle fidelity from proprietary history; user preference for AXIGNAL state over fresh general-AI research; switching cost from investigations, watchlists, dossiers and corrections.
- Falsification: users value only fresh summarisation and do not inspect or revisit structured state.

## 10. H-008 — Deterministic admission

**Claim:** Deterministic structural, rights and epistemic gates materially improve trust and data quality compared with direct model publication.

- State: `SUPPORTED BY PRIOR TECHNICAL THESIS / TECHNICALLY EXERCISED IN AXIGNAL SANDBOX / NOT YET USER-VALIDATED`
- Test: controlled Candidate Claim evaluation measuring false admission, traceability, correction cost and user trust.
- Current evidence: TED parser and lifecycle rehearsals prove models are unnecessary for deterministic observed claims and cannot bypass the blocked actual-source gate.

## 11. H-009 — Regulatory boundary

**Claim:** Material procurement value can be delivered as observation and decision preparation without legal advice, supplier representation, bid execution or guaranteed eligibility and award outcomes.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: user research, legal review and paid usage.
- Falsification: qualified users only pay when AXIGNAL certifies legal compliance, submits bids or guarantees success.

## 12. H-010 — Source economics

**Claim:** Official public procurement sources, deterministic processing, caching and selective model use can support attractive gross margin before expensive commercial data is required.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: source-by-source and ResearchRun-level cost and value ledger.
- Falsification: essential user value requires licences, manual processing or model cost that cannot be supported by validated pricing.

## 13. H-011 — Revenue-gated AI

**Claim:** Deterministic processing, caching and local models can support foundation quality, while paid frontier-model usage can be limited to revenue-backed high-value document and explanation workflows.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Test: quality/cost comparison by workflow, document class and customer plan.
- Falsification: acceptable extraction or explanation requires universal high-cost inference or cost cannot be bounded by ResearchRun.

## 14. H-012 — Global architecture, selective commercialisation

**Claim:** AXIGNAL can maintain a global canonical procurement architecture while launching commercially only by independently admitted source and jurisdiction pack.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED`
- Test: source and entitlement isolation; reversible taxonomy mapping; jurisdiction-specific rights and privacy review; customer demand and economics by geography.
- Falsification: cross-jurisdiction normalisation destroys essential semantics; adapter operations exceed paid value; or source rights make a commercially coherent pack impossible.

## 15. H-013 — Brand

**Claim:** AXIGNAL and `axignal.com` can communicate an authoritative, international intelligence product, while the B2G procurement narrative makes the first commercial job understandable.

- State: `PROPOSED`
- Required evidence: domain control, trademark screening, pronunciation tests, B2G buyer perception and visual-identity testing.
- Repository slug `axignal` is not evidence of public-brand validity.

## 16. H-014 — Controlled seven-day free trial

**Claim:** A seven-day trial can demonstrate one complete evidence-linked procurement value loop and improve qualified paid conversion without creating unacceptable cost, abuse, source-right, privacy or premium-perception risk.

Initial candidate:

- one verified organisation and tenant;
- up to two users;
- admitted European TED scope only;
- three ResearchRuns and two full dossiers;
- hard document, page, export and cost limits;
- no API, bulk redistribution, private connectors, unlimited AI or predictions;
- no silent conversion;
- initial preference for no card;
- read-only expiry and tested deletion.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED / IMPLEMENTATION BLOCKED BY AX-F8-T14`
- Evidence required: server-side entitlement tests; value-loop completion; source drill-down; return visit; explicit paid intent; cost, support and abuse evidence; expiry, deletion and tenant-isolation proof.
- Falsification: seven days is too short despite correct activation; support or cost is structurally excessive; abuse cannot be controlled; conversion remains weak; or the trial makes the paid product appear commodity-grade.

## 17. H-015 — Federated global official-source programme

**Claim:** A source-specific programme using official APIs, OCDS, OData, bulk data and feeds can expand AXIGNAL beyond TED while preserving rights, lifecycle fidelity, source-native taxonomies and acceptable economics.

- State: `TEST_DESIGNED / PRODUCT EXPANSION BLOCKED`
- Catalogue: `data/sources/global-public-procurement-catalogue.v0.1.json`.
- Test order: catalogue; bounded technical probe; independent source admission; lifecycle fidelity; buyer and economic validation; jurisdiction-selective commercial availability.
- Initial candidates include official systems in the UK, US, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Brazil, South Korea, India, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and Nigeria.
- Evidence required: at least one non-TED official technical probe, one independently accepted source profile, reversible taxonomy mapping, qualified customer demand and paid jurisdiction value.
- Falsification: official access or rights are insufficient; quality and lifecycle depth are inadequate; one adapter cannot be maintained economically; or global breadth harms European retention and execution.

## 18. Experiment record requirements

Every hypothesis experiment MUST record:

- hypothesis ID;
- population and recruitment;
- date;
- method;
- predeclared success threshold;
- results;
- limitations;
- cost;
- decision activated;
- new state;
- follow-up hypothesis.

## 19. Governance

- A product feature MUST NOT cite a `PROPOSED` or `TEST_DESIGNED` hypothesis as proven.
- Public marketing MUST not overstate supported scope.
- Rejected and superseded hypotheses remain in history.
- Pricing, trial and jurisdiction decisions MUST link to this register.
- Catalogue inclusion MUST NOT be represented as source admission.
- The register MUST be reviewed at every major commercial, source-admission and global-expansion gate.
