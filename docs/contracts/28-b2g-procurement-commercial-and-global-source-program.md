# 28 — B2G Procurement Commercial and Global Source Program

Version: `0.1.0-candidate`
Status: `NORMATIVE CANDIDATE / COMMERCIAL AND SOURCE-EXPANSION GATED`
Goal ID: `AXIGNAL-GOAL-001`
Decision record: `ADR-013`

## 1. Purpose

This contract governs the first commercial expression of AXIGNAL as a **B2G Public Procurement Intelligence** product and the controlled expansion from European TED data to official public-procurement systems in other jurisdictions.

It defines:

- the B2G narrative and buyer;
- the value AXIGNAL is permitted to promise;
- candidate packaging and pricing bands;
- a safe seven-day free-trial design;
- the global public-procurement source programme;
- the end-to-end development sequence and promotion gates;
- authority, rights, privacy, cost and rollback boundaries.

This contract supplements Contracts 00, 01, 03, 08, 09, 11, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27. For the first commercial procurement wedge, this contract supersedes conflicting legacy references to a low-price individual investment tier, a family-office-first buyer or simultaneous multi-universe launch. Those older hypotheses remain auditable history until they are consolidated by a later contract revision.

Nothing in this contract admits a source, enables billing, grants a model canonical authority or represents product-market fit as proven.

## 2. Canonical commercial category

AXIGNAL's parent category remains:

> **Global Opportunity Intelligence**

Its first commercial implementation category MUST be described as:

> **B2G Public Procurement Intelligence**

Canonical commercial descriptor:

> **Evidence-governed intelligence for organisations that sell to government.**

Canonical outcome statement:

> **Find, qualify and investigate public opportunities with every material conclusion linked to its evidence.**

AXIGNAL MUST NOT be positioned as:

- a cheap tender-alert newsletter;
- a generic public-data search engine;
- an unlimited AI chatbot;
- a bid-writing agency disguised as software;
- a legal opinion engine;
- an automated bid-submission or representation service;
- a system that guarantees eligibility, profitability, award or competitive success.

## 3. B2G meaning and buyer boundary

For AXIGNAL, **B2G** means the commercial relationship in which a business, professional services firm, non-profit or consortium identifies, qualifies, prepares for or manages opportunities to supply goods, services, works or expertise to a public body.

The priority buyer groups are:

1. SMEs and growth companies that sell or intend to sell to government;
2. bid, tender and public-sector sales teams;
3. consultancies and advisers that qualify or support public tenders;
4. contractors, technology suppliers, engineering firms and specialised service providers;
5. corporate market-intelligence and market-entry teams;
6. organisations that need auditable procurement research across jurisdictions.

Public bodies, researchers and transparency organisations MAY use AXIGNAL under separate packaging, but they MUST NOT be treated as the validated primary buyer without evidence.

The buyer is an organisation or professional workflow, not a casual consumer. Initial public copy MUST therefore emphasise professional decision preparation, evidence, coverage and workflow rather than entertainment, conversational novelty or low price.

## 4. Core job to be done

AXIGNAL MUST help a qualified B2G user answer:

- Which official opportunities are relevant to our capabilities and geography?
- Which requirements are observed in the notice or documents?
- Which requirements remain missing, ambiguous, contested or version-dependent?
- What changed across correction, cancellation, award and contract lifecycle events?
- Which buyers, suppliers, classifications, lots, values and regions are connected?
- What does the historical award and participation record show?
- What evidence supports or contradicts pursuing this opportunity?
- What must a human bid, legal, technical or financial specialist still verify?
- Is the expected value of further investigation greater than its cost?

AXIGNAL MUST optimise for a better **pursue / monitor / decline / investigate further** decision. It MUST NOT substitute for the customer's final eligibility, legal, technical, commercial or bid-submission judgment.

## 5. Value architecture

The paid value is not access to public notices by itself. The paid value MUST be expressed through one or more of the following measurable outcomes:

- reduced time from publication to qualified opportunity;
- reduced analyst time spent locating and reconciling source documents;
- avoided pursuit cost on clearly unsuitable or insufficiently evidenced opportunities;
- earlier detection of corrections, cancellations, awards and material changes;
- traceable requirement and evidence review;
- reusable buyer, supplier, lot, classification and award history;
- collaboration around one persistent InvestigationContext;
- auditable dossiers that preserve source, version, claim status and uncertainty;
- cross-jurisdiction discovery through a canonical procurement model;
- faster handoff from market discovery to the customer's bid workflow.

The following MAY increase perceived quality but MUST NOT be used as the primary value proof:

- a visual globe by itself;
- number of AI messages;
- number of generated claims;
- raw database size without coverage quality;
- model brand;
- uncalibrated confidence percentages;
- unsupported claims of prediction or competitive advantage.

## 6. Epistemic and authority boundary

The existing AXIGNAL authority model remains unchanged:

```text
official source object
→ immutable evidence reference
→ deterministic or probabilistic Candidate Claim proposal
→ structural, rights, temporal and epistemic gates
→ admitted, contested, rejected, expired or review-required state
→ InvestigationContext and dossier
```

A model MAY propose extraction, classification, comparison or explanation. A model MUST NOT:

- admit canonical truth;
- invent source pages or clauses;
- assign a customer-facing probability merely from self-reported confidence;
- determine legal compliance or supplier eligibility as fact;
- declare a notice unlawful or deliberately directed to a supplier;
- promise win probability, margin or award outcome before an accepted calibrated model exists under F10.

Structured output validates shape, not truth. Every material procurement conclusion MUST remain recoverable to versioned evidence and an explicit admission state.

## 7. Candidate packaging architecture

Exact names, prices, limits and discounts remain hypotheses until their gates pass. The initial architecture SHOULD use three paid commercial tiers plus a bounded design-partner state.

### 7.1 Design Partner

Purpose: obtain paid workflow evidence before general availability.

Candidate commercial band:

- **€300–€600 per organisation per month**;
- fixed term of three to six months;
- preferential terms exchanged for documented workflow access, feedback, data-quality reports and permission to evaluate decision impact;
- no implication that this is the future public list price.

A Design Partner agreement MUST state:

- admitted source and geography scope;
- included users and ResearchRuns;
- support boundary;
- data and feedback obligations;
- confidentiality and case-study permissions;
- exit and migration treatment;
- which capabilities are experimental.

### 7.2 Professional

Target: one procurement, business-development or advisory professional.

Candidate price band:

- **€349–€499 per month**;
- annual commitment MAY use an equivalent monthly price only when the full annual amount and real saving are disclosed.

Candidate value scope:

- one primary seat;
- admitted European procurement coverage;
- persistent investigations and watchlists;
- bounded monthly ResearchRuns and dossiers;
- source-linked Claim and Evidence Rail;
- change, cancellation and award monitoring within source latency;
- professional export allowance within source rights;
- standard support.

### 7.3 Team / Growth

Target: active bid teams, consultancies and public-sector sales teams.

Candidate price band:

- **€899–€1,499 per month**;
- annual contract SHOULD become the default after willingness-to-pay validation.

Candidate value scope:

- three to five included seats;
- shared InvestigationContexts, Trails, annotations and assignment;
- larger ResearchRun, dossier, watchlist and alert capacity;
- deeper historical comparisons;
- buyer, supplier and award-network analysis where admitted;
- workflow exports and integrations that are technically and legally available;
- priority support.

### 7.4 Enterprise

Target: larger suppliers, consultancies, multi-region teams and governed procurement-intelligence programmes.

Candidate price band:

- **from €18,000 to €45,000 per year** before exceptional source, service or deployment requirements;
- annual contract by default;
- higher quotes MAY apply when private connectors, data licences, API volume, residency, SSO, SCIM, SLA or professional services materially change cost and obligation.

Candidate value scope:

- negotiated seats and organisations;
- enterprise roles and audit logs;
- SSO and SCIM when delivered;
- API and data-feed quotas only for admitted redistributable data;
- private source connectors and tenant-private claims;
- contractual support and security package;
- optional jurisdiction packs;
- explicit service, source-licence and implementation components.

## 8. Premium-perception rules

AXIGNAL MUST remain competitively reachable without signalling commodity value.

The following rules apply:

1. The initial B2G launch MUST NOT use a permanent €19–€99 full-product tier.
2. Public free access, when offered, MUST demonstrate method and evidence but not reproduce the full paid workflow.
3. Price comparison MUST foreground decision capacity, coverage, collaboration, governance and evidence—not artificial feature-count inflation.
4. Discounts MUST NOT become the primary reason to buy.
5. Unlimited AI or unlimited document processing MUST NOT be promised where cost or abuse cannot be bounded.
6. The value metric SHOULD be ResearchRuns, monitored opportunities, dossiers, collaboration and admitted coverage—not tokens.
7. Enterprise pricing MUST separate software, usage, third-party data and professional services when material.
8. A lower price MAY be tested, but only as an explicit hypothesis with premium-perception and retention measures.

## 9. Pricing validation programme

No candidate price becomes `PUBLIC_CURRENT` without evidence.

### 9.1 Required experiments

AXIGNAL MUST conduct at least:

- buyer interviews with people who control or influence a procurement-intelligence budget;
- a paid Design Partner test;
- at least two materially different price or packaging offers;
- a willingness-to-pay exercise based on a working dossier, not concept copy alone;
- gross-margin measurement by activated account;
- support-effort measurement;
- renewal or annual-commitment evidence.

### 9.2 Pricing sweet-spot decision

The selected entry price MUST satisfy all of:

- credible relative to professional B2G tools;
- low enough for a qualified SME or consultancy to purchase without enterprise procurement;
- high enough to fund evidence processing, support and source operations;
- understood as a professional workflow product rather than a commodity alert service;
- associated with a measurable avoided cost or time saving;
- compatible with target gross margin after trial, data, infrastructure and support costs.

### 9.3 Predeclared commercial evidence

Promotion from hypothesis requires evidence including:

- trial-to-paid or design-partner-to-paid conversion;
- paid activation and repeated use;
- renewal or annual commitment;
- observed use of evidence drill-down and dossiers;
- gross margin by plan;
- customer-reported decision impact;
- downgrade, cancellation and support behaviour;
- no dependence on hidden unlimited consulting.

## 10. Seven-day free trial

A seven-day free trial is authorised only as a **candidate controlled acquisition mechanism**. It is not authorised for production until every gate in this section passes.

### 10.1 Trial objective

The trial MUST allow a qualified user to experience one complete value loop:

```text
verified organisation
→ relevant official opportunity
→ persistent ResearchRun
→ source and document processing
→ evidence-linked dossier
→ saved or monitored InvestigationContext
→ explicit paid-plan decision
```

The trial MUST prove the method, not simulate the whole Enterprise product.

### 10.2 Duration and identity

- Duration: seven consecutive 24-hour periods from activation.
- One trial per verified organisation or business domain within a declared cooling-off period.
- Business-email or equivalent organisation verification SHOULD be required.
- Disposable-email and automated-account abuse controls MUST exist.
- One tenant and a maximum of two trial users SHOULD be the initial limit.
- The server MUST resolve tenant, entitlement and expiry; the frontend MUST NOT be the enforcement boundary.

### 10.3 Initial trial allowance

The first production candidate SHOULD be capped at:

- TED / admitted European procurement only;
- three ResearchRuns;
- two full evidence-linked dossiers;
- a bounded document and page allowance declared before execution;
- a bounded number of saved opportunities and alerts;
- one watermarked or trial-labelled professional export if source rights permit;
- standard-frequency updates only.

The exact limits MUST be configurable and measured. A trial action that could exceed its budget MUST stop before cost is incurred or request explicit upgrade.

### 10.4 Trial exclusions

The trial MUST NOT include:

- public or enterprise API access;
- bulk raw-data export or redistribution;
- private connectors;
- SSO, SCIM or enterprise administration;
- unlimited document ingestion or AI;
- high-frequency automation;
- cross-jurisdiction packs not yet product-admitted;
- customer-facing predictive win, margin or eligibility scores;
- bid submission, representation or automated legal conclusions.

### 10.5 Payment and conversion

The initial validation trial SHOULD NOT require a payment card unless evidence demonstrates that card collection materially improves qualified conversion without unacceptable friction or trust damage.

The trial MUST NOT:

- convert silently to paid;
- create a charge without affirmative plan selection;
- use fabricated countdown urgency;
- hide plan limits or renewal terms.

At expiry:

- execution and monitoring MUST stop;
- the workspace SHOULD become read-only for a declared short period;
- the user MUST receive a clear export, upgrade or deletion choice;
- no paid plan starts without explicit agreement.

### 10.6 Retention and deletion

The initial candidate SHOULD retain trial workspace metadata in read-only state for no more than 30 days after expiry unless the user converts, requests earlier deletion or another lawful contractual basis applies.

Trial retention MUST declare:

- what is retained;
- what is deleted;
- whether source-derived objects remain globally available independently of the trial;
- how tenant-private annotations and uploads are deleted;
- when backups expire;
- how the user exercises deletion rights.

### 10.7 Abuse, security and cost controls

Before activation, the trial MUST have:

- server-side entitlements and hard limits;
- organisation and rate-limit controls;
- source-specific rights enforcement;
- request, ResearchRun and export audit events;
- per-trial cost ledger and kill switch;
- prompt and document-size limits;
- malware and unsafe-file controls for any upload;
- tenant isolation tests;
- retention and deletion jobs;
- anomaly detection for automated extraction or redistribution behaviour;
- operator ability to suspend a trial without disabling paid tenants.

### 10.8 Trial promotion gate

The seven-day trial can be promoted beyond a private cohort only when:

1. a qualified user can complete the full value loop without operator data repair;
2. entitlements, expiry and read-only transition are tested end to end;
3. no source, export or attribution right can be bypassed;
4. tenant isolation and deletion tests pass;
5. average activated-trial variable cost fits the approved acquisition model;
6. abuse and support burden remain acceptable;
7. the trial produces credible paid-intent or conversion evidence;
8. no public copy implies unsupported global coverage or predictive authority.

## 11. Global Public Procurement Source Program

The user-facing ambition to incorporate “all TEDs worldwide” MUST be implemented as a **federated official public-procurement source programme**, not as one universal scraper or an immediate claim of global support.

A jurisdiction is not supported merely because its portal is listed, accessible or partially parsed.

### 11.1 Source unit

Every official portal, API, bulk dataset or government feed MUST have its own:

- `source_id`;
- public owner and jurisdiction;
- government level;
- access mechanism;
- official documentation;
- rights and attribution record;
- source state and kill switch;
- parser and normaliser version;
- lifecycle coverage;
- freshness and outage model;
- data-quality profile;
- personal-data classification;
- commercial entitlement constraints;
- operational cost and demand evidence.

Federal, national, regional, state, provincial, municipal and agency systems MUST remain distinguishable even when they share a country.

### 11.2 Adapter classes

AXIGNAL MAY support these adapter classes:

1. official REST, GraphQL, OData or OpenAPI service;
2. official OCDS releases or records API;
3. official scheduled bulk JSON, XML, CSV or RDF distribution;
4. official RSS or incremental notice feed with lawful document retrieval;
5. official portal with documented export but no API;
6. contracted or partner feed;
7. user-authorised tenant connector.

Portal scraping without an admitted legal, contractual and technical basis MUST remain prohibited.

### 11.3 OCDS boundary

The Open Contracting Data Standard MAY serve as an interoperability envelope and canonical-design reference. It MUST NOT be treated as proof that:

- every implementation is complete;
- every country exposes the same lifecycle;
- all fields have equivalent semantics;
- one adapter can safely ingest every OCDS publisher;
- source rights are identical;
- award, implementation and document coverage are present.

Each OCDS publisher still requires independent admission and mapping.

### 11.4 Canonical procurement model

Global sources MUST map into a versioned canonical model that preserves source-native values and can represent at least:

- jurisdiction and government level;
- buyer and procuring entity;
- procedure and notice identifiers;
- notice version and lifecycle event;
- lots;
- title, description and language;
- classification systems and source-native codes;
- place of performance;
- currencies, values and tax treatment;
- participation and eligibility statements;
- deadlines;
- award criteria when available;
- bidders, suppliers, awards and contracts when lawful;
- amendments, cancellations and implementation events;
- documents and evidence references;
- missing, unknown and not-applicable distinctions.

CPV, NAICS, PSC, UNSPSC and national taxonomies MUST NOT be silently collapsed. Crosswalks MUST be versioned, many-to-many, reversible and uncertainty-aware.

### 11.5 Initial source-candidate families

The source catalogue MAY include the following official families as `DISCOVERED`, `TECHNICAL_PROBE` or later states. Listing is not product admission.

#### Europe

- European Union — TED Search API, Open Data Service and eForms XML;
- United Kingdom — Find a Tender and Contracts Finder official services;
- national and subnational European portals only through independent records.

#### North America

- United States — SAM.gov Contract Opportunities and related official federal data services;
- Canada — CanadaBuys official notices, tender and contract-history datasets;
- state, provincial and municipal portals as independent sources.

#### Latin America and Caribbean

- Chile — Mercado Público / ChileCompra official API and datasets;
- Colombia — SECOP official open-data and OData/Socrata services;
- Brazil — Compras.gov.br official open-data and API services;
- further national systems only after official access and rights are verified.

#### Asia

- South Korea — KONEPS official public-data APIs;
- India — Central Public Procurement / eProcure official portal and any verified official data service;
- additional national procurement systems through the same independent gate.

#### Oceania

- Australia — AusTender official notices, awards and published data services;
- New Zealand — Government Electronic Tenders Service and official open-data or feed mechanisms.

#### Africa

- South Africa — eTenders official OCDS API and bulk data;
- Nigeria — NOCOPO official OCDS publication;
- further national and development-bank systems only after admission.

### 11.6 Source priority score

Candidate sources MUST be prioritised using a versioned score containing at least:

- official and stable machine access;
- commercial-use, storage, transformation, display and redistribution rights;
- notice, award, contract and change-lifecycle coverage;
- document availability;
- identifier stability;
- data completeness and update latency;
- taxonomy and language complexity;
- privacy exposure;
- operational cost and rate limits;
- target-buyer demand;
- strategic cross-border value;
- ability to fit the canonical model without semantic fabrication.

No source may be prioritised solely because its country has a large procurement market.

## 12. Global rollout order

Global breadth MUST follow proven end-to-end value, not precede it.

### Stage G0 — Catalogue

Create and maintain the machine-readable inventory of official procurement systems, access methods, rights state and evidence gaps.

Maximum state: `DISCOVERED`.

### Stage G1 — Technical probes

Run bounded, non-persistent probes against selected official services with no public support claim.

Maximum state: `TECHNICAL_PROBE`.

### Stage G2 — Source admission

Complete rights, privacy, parser, normalisation, quality, outage, attribution and revocation gates.

Maximum state: `PRODUCT_ADMITTED` for the exact source and profile only.

### Stage G3 — Canonical lifecycle fidelity

Prove corrections, cancellations, awards, lots, versions and source-native taxonomy semantics against frozen and live evidence.

### Stage G4 — Buyer and economic validation

Prove that the jurisdiction pack creates paid user value and acceptable margin.

### Stage G5 — Commercial availability

Expose the jurisdiction only through explicit entitlements and accurate coverage statements.

The recommended sequence is:

1. complete the European TED product-admission and ResearchRun loop;
2. validate B2G willingness to pay with European design partners;
3. probe the United Kingdom, United States and Canada;
4. admit one additional high-quality OCDS or structured source where demand and rights justify it;
5. expand selectively through Latin America;
6. add Asia, Oceania and Africa by verified source quality and customer demand;
7. add subnational systems only when they materially improve a validated buyer workflow.

## 13. End-to-end development mapping

### F8 — First lawful procurement universe

F8 MUST complete:

```text
TED source admission
→ authenticated identity
→ tenant resolved in server
→ Navigator creates persistent ResearchRun
→ worker processes admitted source and documents
→ Evidence Objects and Candidate Claims
→ deterministic admission and bounded review
→ dossier and claims return to InvestigationContext
→ UI replaces fixtures with governed state
```

No global source expansion may become the next implementation priority before this loop passes its applicable source, security, privacy, cost and usability gates.

### F9 — Paid B2G design-partner product

F9 MUST add:

- organisation, roles and server-side entitlements;
- candidate B2G packages;
- paid Design Partner agreements;
- Stripe billing only after approved commercial and security gates;
- controlled seven-day trial behind a feature flag;
- trial cost, activation, conversion and abuse telemetry;
- at least ten independent paying design partners or an explicitly approved equivalent annual-contract signal.

### F10 — Calibrated procurement scenarios

Supplier win probability, profitability, expected margin, eligibility scoring or other predictive product claims remain disabled until F10 establishes:

- labelled outcomes;
- temporal holdouts;
- declared baselines;
- calibration;
- error and subgroup analysis;
- user-facing uncertainty;
- demotion and retirement rules.

### F11 — Enterprise and API

Enterprise API, private data and security capabilities may be sold only when tenant isolation, source redistribution, quotas, audit and contractual obligations are tested.

### F12 — General availability and global source expansion

Additional jurisdiction packs MUST enter through independent G0–G5 gates. General availability MUST remain jurisdiction-selective and source-specific.

- completed ResearchRuns;
- saved or monitored investigations;
- correction and cancellation detection latency;
- dossier revisit and share rate;

- trial-to-paid conversion;

- annual commitment rate;

- ResearchRun budget rejection count;

## 15. Acceptance gates

This contract moves from candidate to accepted only when:

1. qualified B2G users understand AXIGNAL's category and value without interpreting it as a cheap alert service;
2. the European TED end-to-end loop is product-admitted and evidence-backed;
3. at least one paid pricing experiment tests the declared bands;
4. server-side plan and trial entitlements are reproducible;
5. the seven-day trial passes security, privacy, rights, expiry, deletion, cost and abuse tests;
6. trial or Design Partner evidence demonstrates paid intent and repeated professional use;
7. every marketed plan maps to real entitlements;
8. no predictive or legal claim exceeds admitted authority;
9. the global source catalogue is source-specific and does not imply unsupported country coverage;
10. at least one non-TED source completes a bounded technical probe before any global product claim;
11. every product-admitted source passes Contract 03 independently;
12. rollback and kill switches are tested.

## 16. Explicit exclusions

This contract does not authorise:

- immediate ingestion of every global procurement portal;
- unauthorised scraping;
- a universal adapter that discards source semantics;
- public claims of worldwide coverage;
- billing activation;
- production trial activation;
- API redistribution;
- private customer documents;
- model-written canonical claims;
- win-probability, margin or eligibility prediction;
- bid submission or legal representation;
- replacement of the current TED priority.

## 17. Migration and rollback

This contract is initially documentation, catalogue and task-governance only.

Migration implications:

- no database migration;
- no runtime connector activation;
- no Stripe or entitlement mutation;
- no public pricing change;
- no source-state promotion;
- no customer data migration.

Rollback:

- retire or supersede Contract 28 and ADR-013;
- remove candidate roadmap tasks and source catalogue entries from active planning;
- preserve decision and research history for audit;
- leave TED and every other source in its pre-existing source state;
- keep billing, trial and global connectors disabled.
