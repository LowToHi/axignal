## Purpose

P16 implements a governed evidence and operations surface for patents, research,
funding, institutions, researchers and commercialisation signals. It does not
provide legal opinions, freedom-to-operate conclusions, patent-validity
conclusions or authority to file, license, contact, publish or invest.

## Core truth boundaries

- A patent application is not a granted right.
- A publication is not a current legal-status determination.
- A grant does not establish validity, enforceability or freedom to operate.
- Inventor, author, applicant, assignee, owner and licensee are distinct roles.
- A patent family does not establish ownership or legal equivalence.
- A citation does not establish invalidity, infringement or novelty failure.
- A research publication does not establish replication or commercial readiness.
- A research grant does not establish ownership of resulting IP.
- A licensing signal is not an available or executed licence.
- Person and institution matches remain reversible candidates until reviewed.

## Workspace authority

The workspace may preserve facts, calculations, hypotheses, contradictions,
unknowns, decisions and plans. Models may propose classifications, matches,
landscapes, theses and drafts. They cannot:

- declare a patent granted, valid or enforceable;
- issue freedom-to-operate or infringement conclusions;
- silently merge people, institutions or patent families;
- infer ownership from inventorship, authorship or funding;
- expand researcher profiles beyond lawful source scope;
- file patent applications or accept licence terms;
- contact third parties or publish claims without human authority;
- commit budget, capital or organisational representation.
