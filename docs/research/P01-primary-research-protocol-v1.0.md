# P01 — Primary Research Protocol v1.0

**Task:** `AX-GE2E-P01-T01`  
**Status:** `READY_FOR_RECRUITMENT / NO SESSIONS RECORDED`  
**Purpose:** validate buyer, workflow, budget, failure-cost and purchase-process hypotheses across O01–O09.

## 1. Research questions

1. Which recurring opportunity workflow does the participant operate?
2. What event starts the workflow?
3. Which sources and tools are used?
4. Where does context move between systems?
5. Which steps require evidence, approval or audit?
6. What recent failure occurred and what did it cost?
7. Who owns the budget and purchase decision?
8. What is already paid for?
9. What would make the participant switch or consolidate?
10. Does a connected intelligence-plus-operations product solve a material problem?

AXIGNAL is shown only after the current workflow has been reconstructed.

## 2. Participant qualification

A qualified participant must satisfy all of:

- operates or owns a workflow relevant to at least one opportunity library;
- has completed a real case within the previous 12 months;
- is not affiliated with AXIGNAL;
- can describe current tools and handoffs;
- consents to a sanitised research record.

A budget-qualified participant must additionally:

- approve spend;
- influence the approved budget materially; or
- have completed a recent purchase of a comparable tool.

General interest, futurism and product enthusiasm do not qualify as buyer evidence.

Permitted channels:

- targeted professional outreach;
- industry associations;
- referrals;

- existing professional contacts;

Suggested invitation:

## 5. Session format

Duration: 45–60 minutes.

### Part A — Context, 5 minutes

- role;
- organisation type and size;
- regions;
- library/workflow;
- responsibility and budget authority.

### Part B — Recent case reconstruction, 20 minutes

Prompt:

> Tell me about the most recent real opportunity you evaluated or operated. Start with the event that first brought it to your attention.

Follow-up:

- What happened next?
- Which source did you trust?
- Which tool was used?
- Who received the handoff?
- What document was created?
- Which approval was required?
- Where did the process slow or break?
- What changed later?
- How was the outcome recorded?

### Part C — Failure cost, 10 minutes

- Describe the latest missed, late, duplicated or low-quality decision.
- What direct time or money was lost?
- Was revenue, funding, compliance, project timing or reputation affected?
- How certain is the estimate?
- Who noticed the failure?
- Did it change tooling or process?

### Part D — Existing spend and purchase, 10 minutes

- Which products are paid for?
- Annual or monthly spend band?
- Who approves?
- Procurement/security/legal steps?
- Renewal conditions?
- What would trigger cancellation?
- Which integration is mandatory?
- What data must be exportable?

### Part E — Concept test, 10 minutes

Show the neutral chain:

```text
signals
→ governed evidence
→ investigation
→ opportunity
→ operational workspace
→ outcome
→ learning
```

Ask:

- Which part is valuable?
- Which part is unnecessary?
- Where would AXIGNAL duplicate an existing system?
- What must be present before replacing current tools?
- Which library would be first?
- Which additional library would create expansion value?
- What would make the price indefensible?

Do not ask “Would you buy it?” without a concrete workflow, scope and price.

## 6. Pricing test

Test at least two materially different offers.

Candidate A:

```text
Professional
€349–€499/month
1 library
1–2 users
bounded workspaces and documents
```

Candidate B:

```text
Team
€899–€1,499/month
multiple users
collaboration, approvals, reporting
1–2 libraries
```

Enterprise discussion:

```text
from €18,000/year
security, API, private data and integrations
```

Record:

- current spend;
- budget authority;
- price reaction;
- required ROI proof;
- approval path;
- annual preference;
- expected implementation;
- cancellation trigger.

A hypothetical “acceptable” answer without authority is grade D evidence.

## 7. Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Recent observed case plus documentary or system evidence |
| B | Recent case recalled in detail |
| C | General operating opinion |
| D | Hypothetical reaction |

P01 acceptance uses primarily A and B evidence.

## 8. Privacy and storage

The public repository stores only sanitised records.

Never commit:

- name;
- email;
- phone;
- employer when re-identification is likely;
- confidential tender, grant, regulatory, deal or patent documents;
- recordings;
- raw transcripts;
- credentials;
- customer data.

Private consent and contact records must be stored outside the public repository under restricted access.

Session IDs use `P01-INT-###`.

## 9. P01 gates

### G1 — Coverage

- 45 unique qualified participants;
- all nine libraries reach quota;
- regional and role quotas pass.

### G2 — Recurrence

For each library, at least 60% of qualified participants describe a recurring workflow rather than a one-off curiosity.

### G3 — Fragmentation

For each library, at least 60% use two or more systems with a material handoff.

### G4 — Failure cost

At least three recent material failure incidents per library.

### G5 — Workspace value

At least 60% of direct operators identify value in maintaining active work, documents, decisions or learning inside the product after discovery.

### G6 — Evidence value

At least 50% of participants in each high-consequence cohort identify source traceability, versioning, audit or explicit uncertainty as a purchase criterion.

### G7 — Budget

At least three budget-authoritative signals per library, with current spend, approved budget or recent purchase evidence.

### G8 — Cross-library expansion

At least two cohorts identify a credible second library that would be purchased or used within the same tenant.

### G9 — Negative evidence

At least ten explicit non-buy or rejection cases are documented and incorporated.

### G10 — Saturation

The final sessions in each cohort produce no material new workflow stage, buyer role or failure class.

## 10. Decision rule

P01 may transition to `EVIDENCE_READY` only when G1–G10 pass and an independent gate reviewer confirms that:

- interviews are qualified;
- evidence is not fabricated;
- negative findings are preserved;
- price remains a hypothesis;
- no source or library is admitted;
- P02 inputs are concrete.

Otherwise P01 remains `IN_PROGRESS` or becomes `REJECTED`.
