- `axignal.com` is the canonical domain.
- Navigator is a command and explanation layer, not the product truth.
- Globe and Graph are equal lenses over one InvestigationContext.
- AUTO routing is subordinate to explicit user choice.
- Timeline and Claim/Evidence Rail persist across lens changes.
- English is default; Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Simplified Chinese are launch locales.
- User intent may become private memory or an aggregate Knowledge Tide under purpose-specific controls.

- Knowledge Tides may create research candidates only.
- Development is governed by phases, typed tasks, dynamic skills, evidence and independent gates.
