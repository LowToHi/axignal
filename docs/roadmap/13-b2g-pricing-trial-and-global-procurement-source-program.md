# 13 — B2G Pricing, Trial and Global Procurement Source Program

Version: `0.1.0-candidate`
Status: `CANDIDATE / TED E2E FIRST / COMMERCIAL AND GLOBAL EXPANSION GATED`
Goal ID: `AXIGNAL-GOAL-001`
Governing contract: `28`
Decision: `ADR-013`

## 1. Objective

Turn European Public Procurement Intelligence into AXIGNAL's first evidence-backed B2G product, validate a premium but reachable commercial model, and establish the governed path for later integration of official procurement systems worldwide.

## 2. Non-negotiable order

```text
TED product admission
→ authenticated tenant-scoped ResearchRun E2E
→ qualified B2G workflow validation
→ paid Design Partners
→ controlled seven-day trial
→ validated pricing and retention
→ one non-TED bounded technical probe
→ independent source admission
→ jurisdiction-selective expansion
```

No source catalogue entry, competitor benchmark or technical accessibility may reorder this chain.

## 3. Workstream A — European procurement E2E

Task: `AX-F8-T14`

Required transition:

```text
authenticated identity
→ tenant resolved in server
→ Navigator creates persistent ResearchRun
→ polling or SSE progress
→ worker processes admitted TED evidence
→ Evidence Objects and Candidate Claims
→ deterministic admission and bounded review
→ dossier and claims return to InvestigationContext
→ fixture authority is removed progressively
```

Exit evidence:

- exact TED rights record accepted;
- source reaches `PRODUCT_ADMITTED` only for the tested profile;
- tenant-scoped E2E passes;
- one qualified B2G user completes the value loop;
- cost, latency, quality, rollback and source kill switches pass;
- no model or reviewer receives canonical write authority.

## 4. Workstream B — B2G narrative and paid Design Partners

Task: `AX-F9-T15`

Narrative:

> AXIGNAL is evidence-governed B2G procurement intelligence for organisations that sell to government.

Paid Design Partner hypothesis:

- €300–€600 per organisation per month;
- three-to-six-month term;
- bounded users, ResearchRuns, dossiers and source scope;
- explicit feedback and workflow-validation obligations;
- no public-price implication.

Commercial bands to test:

- Professional: €349–€499/month;
- Team / Growth: €899–€1,499/month;
- Enterprise: €18,000–€45,000/year starting range.

The price decision must optimise for:

- professional credibility;
- SME purchase accessibility;
- measurable value and retention;
- gross margin;
- support burden;
- expansion path;
- no commodity alert positioning.

## 5. Workstream C — Controlled seven-day trial

The trial remains disabled until Workstream A passes and server-side commercial entitlements exist.

Initial candidate:

- seven consecutive days;
- one verified organisation and tenant;
- up to two users;
- admitted European TED scope only;
- three ResearchRuns;
- two complete dossiers;
- hard document, page, export and cost limits;
- no API, bulk export, private connectors, enterprise administration or prediction;
- no silent paid conversion;
- initial preference for no card;
- read-only expiry state;
- declared maximum 30-day tenant-private retention candidate;
- deletion, abuse, rights and isolation tests.

Private-cohort gate:

- full value loop completes without operator data repair;
- no source-right or tenant bypass;
- cost and support burden fit acquisition economics;
- users understand evidence and uncertainty;
- explicit paid intent is observed.

Public-trial gate:

- private-cohort evidence accepted;
- entitlement, payment, expiry, cancellation and deletion E2E accepted;
- product and legal copy reviewed;
- trial kill switch tested;
- premium perception remains intact.

## 6. Workstream D — Global source catalogue

Task: `AX-F12-T10`
Catalogue: `data/sources/global-public-procurement-catalogue.v0.1.json`

The catalogue records official candidates from Europe, North America, Latin America, Asia, Oceania and Africa. Its existence does not authorise ingestion or marketing.

Source progression:

```text
DISCOVERED
→ LEGAL_REVIEW
→ TECHNICAL_PROBE
→ SANDBOX or INTERNAL_ONLY
→ PRODUCT_ADMITTED
→ jurisdiction entitlement
→ commercial availability
```

Each source needs independent:

- official access evidence;
- rights dimensions;
- parser and normaliser;
- source-native taxonomy treatment;
- lifecycle and identifier fidelity;
- data-quality and outage profile;
- privacy review;
- attribution and revocation;
- demand and economic evidence.

## 8. Global canonical-model requirements

Every adapter must preserve:

- jurisdiction and government level;
- source-native identifiers;
- notice version and event lineage;
- lots, awards and contracts;
- buyer and supplier identity;
- native classifications;
- value, currency and tax semantics;
- deadlines and procedure state;
- original language;
- documents and evidence references;
- missing, unknown and not-applicable values.

CPV, NAICS, PSC, UNSPSC and national catalogues require reversible, versioned, many-to-many crosswalks. Semantic similarity can propose mappings but cannot admit them as canonical equivalence.

## 9. Acceptance evidence map

| Gate | Evidence |
|---|---|
| TED E2E | Rights record, tenant E2E tests, trace, security review, qualified-user session, cost metrics |
| B2G narrative | Buyer comprehension and workflow interviews |
| Paid packaging | Independent paid events, repeated use, renewal or annual commitment, margin |
| Seven-day trial | Entitlement, limit, expiry, deletion, abuse, cost and conversion evidence |
| Non-TED probe | Official endpoint, bounded connector test, no persistence or public claim |
| Source admission | Rights, quality, parser, lifecycle, attribution, outage, revocation and cost |
| Jurisdiction launch | Paid demand, entitlement, support, language and coverage disclosure |

## 10. Kill conditions

Pause or revise the work package if:

- B2G users value only commodity alerts;
- the selected entry price produces no paid commitment;
- the seven-day trial cannot demonstrate value or creates excessive support and abuse;
- source rights prevent the required evidence workflow;
- global normalisation destroys source semantics;
- adapter maintenance cost exceeds validated jurisdiction revenue;
- global expansion diverts execution from the European E2E and paid-retention gates;
- users interpret AXIGNAL outputs as guaranteed eligibility, legality, margin or award probability.

## 11. Rollback

- keep TED and each global source independently disabled or revocable;
- disable trial and pricing feature flags without affecting evidence history;
- preserve paid customer entitlements under their contracted version;
- quarantine source-dependent claims when rights or quality fail;
- retain research and rejected hypotheses for audit;
- never silently fall back to scraping or model-generated truth.
