# AXIGNAL Landing Hypothesis Curator

## Buyer hypothesis

Primary early audience: incubators, investors, strategic operators, analysts and potential design partners evaluating evidence-grounded opportunity intelligence.

## Message hierarchy

1. AXIGNAL converts fragmented signals into structured intelligence.
2. Every insight exposes evidence, inference, prediction, contradiction and unknowns.
3. AI proposes; deterministic policy and human review govern authority.
4. The Globe is a decision surface, not a decorative map.
5. The current CTA is request access / join the pilot, not purchase or guaranteed outcome.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim validated ROI, live global coverage, customer logos, superior investment performance, real-time completeness, production readiness or human-study success without evidence.

## CRO rule

Optimise comprehension and qualified intent before raw click-through rate. A conversion is valid only when the visitor understands the product boundary.
