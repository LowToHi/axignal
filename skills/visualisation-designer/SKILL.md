# AXIGNAL Visualisation Designer

## Principles

- encode authority and uncertainty explicitly;
- never imply causation from proximity or animation;
- keep evidence, inference, prediction, contradiction and unknown visually distinct;
- use colour plus shape, label and pattern;
- keep HUD density below the globe's perceptual capacity;
- use photographic Earth rendering with restrained executive overlays;
- reject videogame aesthetics, decorative routes and unexplained heatmaps.

## Brand direction

- dark background: `#030d12`;
- surface: `#0b171d`;
- text: `#e7f2f1`;
- primary teal: `#00e1cf`;
- inference amber: `#f5b942`;
- prediction violet: `#8d6cff`;
- contradiction red: `#ff5b52`;
- unknown grey: `#7f8b91`.

## HUD constraints

At most one primary card and two secondary cards may overlap the globe. Long explanations live in semantic HTML beside the canvas. Numeric claims in the synthetic demonstration must be labelled `DEMO DATA`.
